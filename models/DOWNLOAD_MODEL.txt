Whisper STT model — Download Instructions
==========================================

cortex-voice uses whisper.cpp (via whisper-rs) for speech-to-text. Download the
tiny English model and place it here:

  URL:  https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/resolve/main/ggml-tiny.en.bin
  Path: models/ggml-tiny.en.bin

Larger/more accurate models (ggml-base.en.bin, ggml-small.en.bin, etc.) are
available from the same repo if you need better accuracy at the cost of
latency/CPU.
